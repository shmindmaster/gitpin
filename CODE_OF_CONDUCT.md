# Code of conduct

RepoContext welcomes people of every background and experience level. Participation in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, and related community spaces requires respectful, professional conduct.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, patient, and respectful.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing perspectives.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for harmful impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct violates these standards. Enforcement will consider context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately using the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue about a conduct incident. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical, without retaliation against good-faith reporters.
